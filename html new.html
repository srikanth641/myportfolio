05Featured Project

AGV Forklift, from brief to build.

Ongoing team project

AGV Forklift

Automation Embedded Sensors Manufacturing
01

Problem Statement

Manual pallet and material movement on the shop floor was slowing throughput.

02

Research

Studied AGV navigation methods, drive layouts and existing lift mechanisms.

03

Concept

Mecanum-wheeled chassis with a scissor lift deck for pallet handling.

04

CAD

Full assembly modelled in SolidWorks — frame, lift linkage, drive mounts and deck.

05

Manufacturing

Frame members cut, drilled and finished in the department project laboratory.

06

Assembly

Drive train, linkage and deck assembled and aligned for repeatable lift travel.

07

Electronics

Motor drivers, sensing and power distribution wired onto the chassis.

08

Programming

Embedded control for drive direction, lift actuation and safe stop behaviour.

09

Testing

Load, travel and lift trials, iterating on stability and clearance.

10

Results

A working prototype answering the company's brief, demonstrated to the automation team.

11

Future Scope

Autonomous navigation, fleet coordination and factory-level Industry 4.0 integration.

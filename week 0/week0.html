Week 0
Activities.

My journey

Week 0 at Forge was mainly about orientation, self-discovery, teamwork, and understanding the mindset needed for the journey ahead.

Orientation Program: We were introduced to Forge, its objectives, culture, mentors, and the overall journey. It helped us understand what to expect and gave us an opportunity to interact with other participants.

Spaghetti Structure Activity: We were given spaghetti and other simple materials and asked to build a structure as a team. It was a fun activity, but it also taught us the importance of teamwork, communication, creativity, planning, and problem-solving. We understood that even a simple task requires proper coordination.

16 Personalities Test: We undertook the 16Personalities assessment to understand our personality traits and working style. My result was Logistician (ISTJ). It helped me understand that I naturally tend to be responsible, practical, organized, detail-oriented, and systematic.

Zen Pencils: We were introduced to Zen Pencils, where simple illustrations and stories were used to communicate meaningful life lessons. We discussed the stories and related their messages to our own experiences. This made the session more personal and helped us look at everyday situations from a different perspective.

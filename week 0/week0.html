My journey

Week 0 marked the beginning of my journey at Forge, where I was introduced to the organization, its work culture, learning approach, and the overall structure of the internship program. The orientation program helped me understand what to expect throughout the upcoming weeks and how the internship would focus not only on technical knowledge but also on teamwork, communication, problem-solving, and personal development.

As part of the orientation, we participated in a team-building activity where we were given a large quantity of spaghetti and asked to build a structure using the available materials. Although the activity appeared simple, it challenged us to think creatively, communicate effectively with our teammates, divide responsibilities, and build a stable structure within the given constraints. It helped me understand the importance of collaboration, planning, experimentation, and learning from failures.

We also undertook the 16 Personalities assessment to understand our individual personality traits, strengths, and working preferences. My result was Logistician (ISTJ). The assessment gave me an opportunity to reflect on my approach to work, responsibility, organization, and problem-solving. It also helped me identify areas where my natural strengths could contribute to a team and areas where I could improve.

Overall, Week 0 was not just an introduction to the internship. It was the starting point for understanding how I work, how I collaborate with others, and how I approach challenges. These early experiences set the foundation for the technical and professional learning that followed in the upcoming weeks.

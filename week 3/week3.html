Week 3 – Electronics, Fusion 360 & Creative Fabrication

Week 3 was a combination of electronics, CAD design, hands-on activities, and creative learning. We were introduced to the basics of electronics and gained practical experience by performing experiments using different electrical and electronic components. This helped us understand basic circuits and their practical applications.

We also learned Fusion 360 in greater depth, covering the essential tools and features required for 3D modelling. As a hands-on task, I designed and modelled a component using Fusion 360, which improved my confidence in CAD modelling and design.

As part of a fun and interactive activity, we designed and launched paper rockets. I also created the rocket design in Fusion 360 before building and testing it. Along with this, we explored clay modelling, where I created a rocket using clay and later designed the same concept digitally in Fusion 360.

The week concluded with an evaluation, where our learning and practical work were assessed. I was awarded a score of 97 marks, reflecting my overall performance and understanding of the activities completed during the week.

Key Learning: Electronics basics, practical experiments, Fusion 360 modelling, product design, paper rocket design and testing, clay modelling, and hands-on problem solving.

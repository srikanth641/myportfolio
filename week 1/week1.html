My journey

In Week 1 at Forge, we focused on developing practical workplace skills and building our professional identity. One of the major learning sessions was 5S methodology, where we learned about Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain.

We did a hands-on 5S activity, which helped us understand how proper organization, cleanliness, standardization, and discipline can improve efficiency and productivity in a workplace. Instead of learning 5S only through theory, the practical activity helped us understand how these principles can be applied in real industrial environments.

Along with this, we also learned how to build our own professional portfolio by presenting our skills, experiences, projects, and achievements in an organized way. We were introduced to GitHub and learned how to upload, manage, and host our portfolio online. This gave me a better understanding of how to showcase my technical journey digitally and helped me take the first step toward building a strong professional presence.
